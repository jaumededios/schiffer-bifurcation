III. THE LIMIT

Why the zoom matters

The landing points crowd the cone.

Each orange point is a fractional-order coincidence found by the phase model. The dashed wall is integer order—the place where the conical lift becomes a smooth planar object.

Finding hits arbitrarily close to that wall supplies the small gap that the bifurcating branch can cross. Geometry and spectral arithmetic meet at the same integer.

How to read this

Intuition first, theorem second.

The geometry is a deliberately stylized model of the Lyapunov–Schmidt branch. Its quadratic radius drop mirrors R(s) = R₀ − ½Γ(λ)s² + O(s³).

The coincidence plots are computed live from the two leading Bessel phase laws used in the density argument: j₁,ₙ ≈ π(n + ¼) and N f(jₙ,ₘ/N) ≈ π(m − ¼), where f(c)=√(c²−1)−acos(1/c).

They explain the mechanism and are not a numerical certificate for any particular exact zero.

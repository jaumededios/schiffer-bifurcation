What is being computed

The interior equation is exact.

Every retained sine and cosine mode solves the Helmholtz equation analytically. Least squares is used only on the moving wall, where the Dirichlet and normal-derivative equations compete.

How to read the picture

The right edge is the unknown.

The vertical direction is the periodic angle θ. The horizontal direction runs inward along the cylinder. Everything to the right of the bright boundary lies outside the domain.

As λ approaches 4, the k = 2 correction decays more slowly: its natural length 1 / √(4 − λ) becomes large. This part is a genuine feature of the cylinder mode equation.

Numerical method

Exact inside, numerical on the wall.

We set Γs: x = hs(θ) = s cos(θ − φ). The field is a truncated sum of separated solutions of (Δ + λ)u = 0 in the physical cylinder coordinates.

The k = 0 and k = 1 radial factors are oscillatory. For k ≥ 2 they are e√(k²−λ)x cos(kθ) and e√(k²−λ)x sin(kθ), which decay as x → −∞. Consequently, the interior PDE residual is identically zero.

For the selected (λ,s), a column-scaled QR solve fits the boundary data using at least 256 angular samples. Exponential modes are rescaled by constants to avoid overflow; this does not change their span. The reported defects are evaluated on a separate grid of at least 512 shifted samples in the normalized metric ||f||² = (2π)⁻¹∫|f(θ)|²dθ. Independent sliders generally move off the true one-dimensional branch, so these defects need not vanish.

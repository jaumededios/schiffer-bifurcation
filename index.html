What is being computed

The field adapts to the moving wall.

The base normal profile is corrected by exponentially decaying sine and cosine modes. Their coefficients are solved by least squares against the Helmholtz residual on the current variable domain.

How to read the picture

The right edge is the unknown.

The vertical direction is the periodic angle θ. The horizontal direction runs inward along the cylinder. Everything to the right of the bright boundary lies outside the domain.

As λ approaches 4, the k = 2 correction decays more slowly: its natural length 1 / √(4 − λ) becomes large. This part is a genuine feature of the cylinder mode equation.

Numerical method

A small Galerkin calculation, in your browser.

We set Γs: x = hs(θ) = s cos(θ − φ) and use the inward distance d = hs(θ) − x. The base field is cos(√λ d).

The critical k = 1 corrector is oscillatory. The higher correctors are d²e−qₖd cos(kθ) and d²e−qₖd sin(kθ), with qₖ = √(k² − λ) for k ≥ 2. The factor d² makes both boundary conditions exact for every fitted coefficient.

At every interaction, the site samples the variable domain and solves a ridge-regularized least-squares system minimizing ||(Δ + λ)u||₂. This is a genuine truncated numerical collar model. It is not a proof that the prescribed wall is an exact global free-boundary solution.
